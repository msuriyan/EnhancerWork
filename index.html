EnhancerWork

Four single-file, in-browser tools for making publication figures from comparative and functional genomics data. Nothing to install, and your files are read only by your own browser and never uploaded.

Tool 1

Cactus Alignment Viewer

Cactus whole-genome alignments (bigMaf/MAF) drawn alongside the species phylogeny, ATAC-seq signal and peaks, BED regions and GFF3/GTF gene models.

Open Docs
Tool 2

ATAC Comparative Panels

Multi-species ATAC-seq panels (bigWig + peaks) with tissue overlays, plus homologous-peak tracing across species through a Cactus alignment.

Open Docs
Tool 3

TFBS Enhancer Map

Strand-aware, TRANSFAC-style maps of predicted transcription-factor binding sites (JASPAR, FIMO…) on enhancers and cis-regulatory elements.

Open Docs
Tool 4

GenoView

An IGV/UCSC-style browser for your genome, BAM, bigWig and BED files, with indel/SV detection and CRISPR edit analysis in individual crispants.
